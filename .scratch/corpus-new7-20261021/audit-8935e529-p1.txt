Market-oriented plan #6: price hike of four SKUs at 42 direct self-operating stores incl. 
Feitian; Buy

12m Price Target: Rmb1,667.00 Price: Rmb1,348.86 Upside: 23.6% 
What’s New: Kweichow Moutai’s share price reacted positively on 
Aug 10 by 3% post the news of raising prices for four products 
across its 42 self-operating stores across regions on Aug 8: 
Feitian/Five-star Feitian/Zodiac/Jingpin Moutai are now priced at 
Rmb1,753/1,743/1,951/2,410 per bottle, up 2-3% vs. prior prices, 
with Feitian priced at 7% higher than normal suggested RSP at 
Rmb1,639 on i-Moutai, and also 2% higher vs the latest hiked group 
buy price in self-operated stores at Rmb1,719. For Feitian, the move 
effectively establishes differentiated online/offline pricing, with 
offline self-operated stores priced at Rmb1,753 vs. Rmb1,639 on 
i-Moutai, allowing Moutai to better capture demand from 
corporate/group-buy customers while maintaining a benchmark 
retail price online. 
Our view: We see the move as another constructive step in Moutai’s 
market-oriented reform and pricing mechanism enhancement. The 
combination of broader retail access may help improve price 
transparency, reduce arbitrage across channels and allow Moutai to 
better capture end-consumer demand with a stable pricing level (as 
self-operating store target is almost fulfilled and may more act like a 
pricing benchmark). Since the July price hike, Feitian wholesale 
prices have recovered to above c.Rmb1,700/bottle level 
(Rmb1,710-1,730/bottle in the first week of Aug) and remained 
resilient, supporting improving channel expectations ahead of the 
Mid-Autumn Festival. 
That said, we think the contribution from this round of price hikes 
would be very insignificant given the limited volume offerings and 
higher price for Feitian in this channel vs other channels. More 
importantly, we expect 2H26 Feitian supply to tighten, making price 
stabilization rather than volume growth as the key operating focus 
for Feitian in 2H26 to support wholesale prices into peak seasons.
