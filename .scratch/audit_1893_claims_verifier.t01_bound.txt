How AI video generators work, and how we rank them
Most AI video generators are diffusion transformers. They start from random noise across many frames at once and refine it step-by-step into a coherent clip — guided by a text encoder that converts your prompt into a vector the model can follow. The hard part isn't any single frame; it's temporal consistency: keeping subjects, lighting, and camera motion coherent over time.

How do AI video generators work?
Most AI video generators are diffusion transformers. They start from random noise across many frames at once and refine it step-by-step into a coherent clip — guided by your prompt encoded by a text model. The hard part isn't any single frame; it's temporal consistency: keeping subjects, lighting, and camera motion coherent over time. The best AI video generators get this right even on long clips with multi-subject motion.
Are AI video generators free?
Some are. A handful of open-weight AI video generators (like CogVideoX and Mochi) can be run locally for free if you have GPU access, and most hosted providers (Runway, Kling, Luma, Pika) offer a small free tier. Frontier models like Veo and Sora are paid — typically $0.10 to $1+ per generation depending on duration and resolution. The leaderboard shows live per-clip pricing so you can compare paid vs free AI video generators directly.
Which AI video generator is best for marketing or social content?
For polished short-form clips (Instagram Reels, TikTok, ads), sort the leaderboard by arena score and pick from the top of the text-to-video table — those are the AI video generators that win the most blind human votes on real prompts. If you need to start from a specific brand asset or product photo, switch to the image-to-video tab; if you're modifying existing footage, use the video editing arena.
How are AI video generators ranked?
Each prompt in the Video Arena generates 4 clips from randomly sampled models. Users watch them and pick the best and worst — without seeing model names or providers. Rankings use TrueSkill (conservative score: μ − 3σ) so a model needs many wins to rise. This eliminates brand bias and ensures rankings reflect actual generation quality, not marketing or cherry-picked demos.
Can AI video generators produce long videos?
Today, most produce 5 to 10-second clips. Some models support up to 20 or 60 seconds. For longer content, the standard workflow is to generate multiple clips and stitch them together — using a consistent reference image or prompt seed to keep subjects looking the same across cuts. Length, resolution, and motion complexity all affect generation time and cost.

其核心亮点如下：
单次生成时长达 30 秒，支持多轮延长：Seedance 2.5 可单次生成 30 秒高质量视频片段，并支持多轮延长。同时，模型优化了镜头衔接和场景过渡，让长视频保持更好的连贯性。模型还大幅优化了画质、音质以及运动质量，并有效弱化了视频生成中常见的“油腻感”。用户可以生产数分钟具有统一视听语言、高质量的连贯内容，精彩故事一次呈现。

更精准、稳定的编辑能力：Seedance 2.5 支持精准的时间戳控制，可定向编辑视频内容，提升创作的效率和可控性。同时，模型强化了绿幕编辑、视角编辑、参考编辑等多种编辑能力，满足影视、广告等更多专业、复杂场景的创作需求。
